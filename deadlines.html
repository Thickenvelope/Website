Typical college admissions deadlines by month

Rule Of Thumb

Submit three days early

Application portals slow down and occasionally fail on deadline night. A three-day buffer costs you nothing and removes the worst-case scenario entirely.

Rule Of Thumb

Check the time zone

"Due January 1" usually means 11:59pm in the college's local time zone. If you are three hours behind, that is 8:59pm for you.

Rule Of Thumb

Recommenders need a month

Teachers write dozens of these. Ask at least four weeks out and give them your resume — the letter will be noticeably better.
